Assessment Administration Guide

Consistent conditions, accommodations, scoring, corrections, retesting, and portfolio retention.

Recommended conditions

  • Midterm: 90–110 minutes; one short break permitted.
  • Final: 150–180 minutes or two supervised sessions.
  • Provide scratch paper and an approved reference sheet.
  • Record accommodations without lowering the learning target.
  • The root calculator remains under development and is not an approved course dependency.

Definitive-answer policy

  • Exactly one correct option per item.
  • No equivalent duplicate forms.
  • Restrictions, units, rounding, and conventions are stated when material.
  • Corrections identify the original error, correct reasoning, and verification.
  • Retests should use parallel rather than identical items.

Portfolio evidence

Retain the result, representative scratch work, corrections, unit mastery records, capstone rubric, and course verification record. This package does not replace jurisdiction-specific graduation, end-of-course testing, transcript, evaluation, or accreditation requirements.
