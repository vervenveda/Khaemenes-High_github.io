# Standards Research Notes

The scope was built from the recurring expectations across current official Algebra I and high-school mathematics frameworks:

- Florida B.E.S.T. Algebra 1 and Mathematical Thinking and Reasoning
- Florida Algebra 1 end-of-course resources
- Texas Algebra I TEKS
- New York Next Generation Algebra I
- Virginia 2023 Algebra I Standards of Learning
- California Algebra I and Integrated Mathematics I framework
- Common Core high-school Number, Algebra, Functions, Statistics, Geometry, and modelling
- international-style mathematical modelling, communication, reflection, and data ethics

The course deliberately adds integrated geometry, financial mathematics, regression and residual analysis, proof and error analysis, technology judgment, sensitivity, fairness, and an oral portfolio defence.
