Rational Expressions, Equations & Functions

How do excluded values, asymptotes, holes, and rates of change shape rational expressions and models?

What the learner will study

  • Simplifying Rational Expressions & Restrictions
  • Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions
  • Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions
  • Solving Rational Equations & Extraneous Solutions
  • Reciprocal Functions, Asymptotes & Holes
  • Transformations and Features of Rational Functions
  • Direct, Inverse, Joint & Combined Variation
  • Rational Models, Rates & Work Problems

How to support without taking over

  • Ask the learner to define inputs, outputs, units, and domain.
  • Ask which property or theorem justifies each transformation.
  • Request an independent check by substitution, graph, table, or alternate method.
  • Encourage correction notes rather than erasing evidence of learning.
  • Use the calculator after the mathematical setup is visible.

Evidence to preserve

Keep one worked example, one correction, one application or project artifact, the mastery result, and a reflection explaining growth and next steps.

Applied investigation

Rational Function Design & Rate Model asks the learner to define a real question, construct mathematics, validate results, state uncertainty, and communicate a defensible conclusion.
