# Assessment Model

## Principle

Mastery is inferred from a body of evidence, not one score. Interactive multiple-choice evidence is combined with written reasoning, correction work, projects, modelling, oral explanation, and portfolio samples.

## Evidence sequence

- Readiness diagnostic
- Weekly retrieval and classwork
- Detailed lesson completion
- Foundation/Core/Extended practice
- Unit mastery checks
- Correction logs
- Applied investigations
- Midterm
- Comprehensive final
- Advanced modelling exploration
- Oral defense and revision
- Portfolio and completion record

## Corrections

For every missed or weak item, the learner should record:
1. the original approach;
2. the first invalid step or misconception;
3. the correct governing rule;
4. the revised solution;
5. an independent verification;
6. a brief prevention strategy.

## Retakes

A retake follows review or reteaching. Preserve first-attempt evidence and best evidence when useful. New randomized sets reduce memorization.

## Accommodations

Access supports may include extended time, reduced visual load, enlarged print, oral directions, breaks, alternative input, and separate setting. Supports should remove barriers without changing the mathematical target.
