Recommended build order

Complete the common sequence first, then expand alternatives.

  1. Algebra I
  2. Geometry
  3. Algebra II
  4. Statistics & Probability
  5. Precalculus & Trigonometry
  6. Calculus
  7. Applied and college-readiness electives

Trigonometry is shown with Precalculus because modern high-school precalculus normally includes substantial trigonometric study. A separate Trigonometry mini-course can still be added later for review, acceleration, or technical applications.

Flexible progression

Different pathways without limiting potential.

Students can move among foundation, core, advanced, data, college-readiness, and applied mathematics while maintaining a coherent four-year record.

Foundation Pathway

Strengthens number sense, arithmetic fluency, algebra readiness, mathematical language, and confidence before advancing.

Core Pathway

Provides a complete high-school sequence in algebra, geometry, functions, statistics, and applied problem solving.

Advanced Pathway

Extends into trigonometry, precalculus, calculus, data science, advanced statistics, and college readiness.

Applied Mathematics

Connects mathematics to finance, engineering, technology, architecture, business, science, and everyday decisions.

Intervention & Recovery

Offers targeted review, corrections, alternative explanations, retakes, and mastery-based progress.

Honors & Enrichment

Provides deeper reasoning, proof, modeling, challenge problems, projects, and independent investigation.

Shared mathematics resources

Tools for learning, practice, and documentation.
