Course design

One shared core with flexible support.

Pathways apply by skill. A student may use Foundation support for fractions, Core work for equations, and Extended challenges for geometry.

Foundation

Concrete models, vocabulary support, smaller steps, additional examples, and confidence-building practice.

Core

The complete pre-algebra sequence required for strong progression into Algebra I.

Extended

Proof, modelling, coding connections, unfamiliar problems, research, and deeper mathematical communication.

Course outcomes

  • Reason fluently with integers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, rates, exponents, and roots.
  • Translate between verbal, numeric, symbolic, tabular, graphical, and geometric representations.
  • Solve and check expressions, equations, inequalities, formulas, and proportional relationships.
  • Use data, probability, measurement, and modelling to make defensible decisions.
  • Explain mathematical reasoning, identify assumptions, and evaluate whether results are reasonable.
