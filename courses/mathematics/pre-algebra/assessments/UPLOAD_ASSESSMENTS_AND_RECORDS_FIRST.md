# Upload Assessments & Completion Records

1. Upload the complete `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/assessments/` folder.
2. Upload the complete `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/records/` folder.
3. Replace `courses/mathematics/pre-algebra/index.html`.
4. Replace root `README.md`, `manifest.webmanifest`, and `service-worker.js`.
5. Add `docs/releases/ASSESSMENT_RECORDS_RELEASE.md` and `docs/audits/ASSESSMENT_RECORDS_VALIDATION.md`.
6. Commit the changes to the main branch.

Do not replace Units 01–13, Grade 9, or the diagnostic.
