    Expression Structure Lab

    Change coefficients and constants, then compare equivalent forms.

    Build a linear expression, evaluate it, distribute a grouped form, and inspect how coefficients and constants control output.

    Expression3x+4
    Substitution3(5)+4
    Output19
    Equivalent grouped form3(x+1.333)
    Change when x rises by 1+3
    Constant output at x=04

    The coefficient controls constant change; the constant is the output when x=0.
