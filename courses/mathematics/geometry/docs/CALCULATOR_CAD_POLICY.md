# Calculator, CAD, and Arcade Policy

Tools are used strategically.

## Calculator

Use for trigonometric values, radicals, measurement, and verification when permitted. Show formulas, substitutions, units, and interpretation.

## CAD

Use for accurate construction, scale, dimensions, layers, area/length analysis, technical outputs, and design validation. CAD is not a substitute for proof; exported evidence must be accompanied by mathematical explanation.

## Arcade laboratories

Games and studios are used as investigations. A valid course artifact includes a screenshot/export or pattern description, named geometric relationships, calculations or proof, and reflection.

Formal assessments specify whether tools are allowed.
