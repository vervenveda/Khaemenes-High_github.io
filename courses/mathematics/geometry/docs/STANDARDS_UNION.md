# Geometry Standards Union

This course is not restricted to one state sequence. It was designed from the common and advanced expectations found across current official high-school geometry resources.

## Sources reviewed

- Florida Department of Education B.E.S.T. Mathematics Standards and current Geometry resources/EOC materials
- Texas Education Agency High School Mathematics TEKS, Geometry
- California Mathematics Framework, high-school traditional pathway and Geometry discussion
- Virginia Department of Education 2023 Mathematics Standards of Learning, Geometry
- New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards and Geometry resources
- Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, High School Geometry

## Union of expectations

The course includes:

- precise definitions, constructions, logic, theorem use, and proof
- transformations as a foundation for congruence and similarity
- triangle congruence, centers, inequality, and proof
- parallel/perpendicular relationships and coordinate proof
- dilations, similarity, scale drawings, and indirect measurement
- right triangles, Pythagorean relationships, trigonometry, elevation, depression, and bearings
- polygons, quadrilaterals, regular polygons, and decomposition
- circle theorems, arcs, chords, tangents, secants, sectors, and equations
- loci, distance conditions, intersections, and conic connections
- area, precision, error, geometric probability, and optimization
- solids, nets, cross-sections, surface area, volume, and scaling
- modelling, technology, communication, validation, revision, and design defense

## Beyond minimum standards

The course adds systematic print design, CAD drafting, vector export, mathematical-art laboratories, portfolio evidence, three learning pathways, local-first records, and a capstone defense.
