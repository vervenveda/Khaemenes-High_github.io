# Khaemenes Open-Age Geometry & Spatial Design — Curriculum Map

**Course code:** KH-MATH-GEO  
**Length:** 36 weeks / 180 study sessions  
**Architecture:** 13 units, 90 detailed visual printable lessons, 90 teacher keys, 13 mastery checks, 13 investigations, diagnostic, midterm, final, capstone.

## Course purpose

This open-age, placement-based course joins synthetic geometry, transformation geometry, coordinate methods, measurement, spatial reasoning, technical drawing, CAD, mathematical art, proof, and modelling. Learners progress by evidence rather than age or grade label.

## Week 18 midpoint

Week 18 is reserved for cumulative synthesis, portfolio conference, the 60-question midpoint examination, and written corrections.
