# Assessment Model

- Readiness diagnostic: 40 questions
- Unit mastery checks: 13 × 12 questions
- Midterm: 60 questions, Units 1–6
- Final: 100 questions, all units
- Applied investigations: 13
- Lesson evidence: 90 printable worksheets
- Capstone: geometric model/design, technical portfolio, presentation, defense, revision

The target is 80%, but scores are not the sole evidence. Corrections, proof quality, construction accuracy, projects, oral explanation, and portfolio artifacts complete the record.
