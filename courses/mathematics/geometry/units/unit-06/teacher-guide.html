Teacher guide

Right Triangles, Trigonometry & Bearings

Suggested sequence

  1. Pythagorean Theorem & Its Converse
  2. Special Right Triangles: 45-45-90
  3. Special Right Triangles: 30-60-90
  4. Geometric Mean & Right-Triangle Altitudes
  5. Sine, Cosine & Tangent Ratios
  6. Inverse Trigonometry & Angle Measure
  7. Angles of Elevation and Depression
  8. Bearings, Vectors & Multi-Step Navigation

Instructional priorities

  • Begin with a visible figure and establish notation before abstraction.
  • Require learners to distinguish a measured observation from a proven relationship.
  • Use Foundation, Core, and Extended routes without labeling learners permanently.
  • Print student worksheets and keep teacher keys separate.
  • Use the Arcade and CAD connections as mathematical laboratories with written evidence.

Assessment

Use lesson work, oral reasoning, the 12-question mastery check, corrections, and the project rubric. Mastery target: 80%.
