# Unit 06: Right Triangles, Trigonometry & Bearings

Weeks: 14, 15, 16, 17

Essential question: How do right-triangle relationships turn inaccessible lengths, heights, angles, and directions into measurable quantities?

Applied investigation: Navigation, Height & Bearing Survey

This unit contains 8 visual printable lessons, three pathway practice sets, one mastery check, a family guide, a teacher guide, standards and vocabulary maps, and an applied investigation.
