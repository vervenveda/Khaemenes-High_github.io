Family guide

CAD, Scale Drawing & Spatial Design

How can precise digital drafting turn geometric constraints into measurable, printable, and defensible designs?

What learners will do

Study 7 visual lessons, complete printable practice, explain diagrams and proof, and produce the applied investigation OHMIC CAD Spatial Design Portfolio.

How to support

  • Ask the learner to point to the diagram evidence before explaining a calculation.
  • Encourage exact labels, units, and written reasons.
  • Use household objects, maps, floor plans, shadows, or packaging when helpful.
  • Celebrate corrections as evidence of learning.

Mastery evidence

Target: 80% plus completed corrections and a satisfactory project or portfolio artifact.
