# Khaemenes Open-Age Geometry & Spatial Design

A complete 36-week, placement-based high-school Geometry course designed for national and international use.

## Course totals

- 36 weeks
- 180 study sessions
- 13 units
- 90 detailed visual printable lessons
- 90 separate teacher answer keys
- 13 unit mastery checks
- 13 applied investigations
- 40-question readiness diagnostic
- 156-question validated course bank
- 60-question midterm
- 100-question final
- Geometry modelling capstone and defense
- Printable completion record
- Foundation, Core, and Extended pathways
- Local-first learner records

## Distinctive integrations

- OHMIC Foundry™ Protected CAD Studio
- Sacred Geometry Sanctuary
- Mandala Rings
- Pattern Pirates
- Khaemenes Scientific Calculator v4
