Administration Guide

Mastery model

The course target is 80%. A score is one piece of evidence; written corrections, construction quality, projects, and oral explanation complete the record.

Conditions

  • Provide quiet time and scratch paper.
  • Allow documented accommodations without lowering the mathematical target.
  • Use calculator or CAD only when the task permits it.
  • Retain printed work or exported evidence with the local score record.
  • Require corrections that identify the error, correct method, and transferable lesson.

Integrity

Because questions are generated from a static local bank, teachers should vary attempts and require visible reasoning for formal credit.
