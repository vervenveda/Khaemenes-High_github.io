# Units 01–04 Cumulative Benchmark Study Guide

## Structure

- 60 objective questions: 15 per unit
- Four constructed responses
- Objective mastery: 48/60
- Recommended final mastery: 80/100
- Calculator permitted; corrections and retakes permitted

## Unit 01 — Inquiry, Measurement and Evidence

Review testable questions, variables, controls, operational definitions, SI units, tool resolution, accuracy, precision, repeats, mean, range, tables, graphs, anomalies, correlation, causation, CER, source credibility, safety, and ethical data handling.

## Unit 02 — Cells and Organization

Review cell theory, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, organelles, microscopy, magnification, millimeter–micrometer conversion, membranes, diffusion, osmosis, active transport, percent mass change, surface-area-to-volume ratio, specialization, organization, photosynthesis, respiration, and model limits.

## Unit 03 — Body Systems and Homeostasis

Review functional ranges, negative and positive feedback, receptors, coordinators, effectors, circulation, gas exchange, exercise calculations, digestion, absorption, excretion, ADH, thermoregulation, insulin, glucagon, nervous and endocrine coordination, immunity, evidence quality, privacy, and non-diagnostic data.

## Unit 04 — Division and Inheritance

Review genome, chromosome, DNA, gene, allele, genotype, phenotype, cell cycle, S phase, mitosis, cytokinesis, differentiation, checkpoints, asexual and sexual reproduction, meiosis, homologues, chromatids, crossing over, assortment, fertilization, dominance patterns, Punnett probability, sampling variation, mutation, environment, consent, privacy, and rejection of genetic ranking.

## Calculations

- Mean = sum ÷ number of values
- Range = largest − smallest
- Percent change = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100
- Magnification = image size ÷ actual size
- Actual size = image size ÷ magnification
- 1 mm = 1000 micrometers
- Cube surface area = 6s²; cube volume = s³
- Expected count = probability × sample size
