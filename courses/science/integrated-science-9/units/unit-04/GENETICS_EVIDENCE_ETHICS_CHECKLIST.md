# Genetics Evidence and Ethics Checklist

- [ ] Assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Probability is not presented as certainty.
- [ ] Genotype is not treated as destiny.
- [ ] Environmental and developmental influences are considered.
- [ ] Observed and expected results are distinguished.
- [ ] Personal family genetics are not required.
- [ ] Consent and privacy are protected.
- [ ] Human value is never ranked by genes.
- [ ] Disability, ancestry, sex, gender, culture, and identity are treated respectfully.
- [ ] Historical misuse, including eugenics, is recognized.
- [ ] Benefits, risks, alternatives, equity, and uncertainty are evaluated.
