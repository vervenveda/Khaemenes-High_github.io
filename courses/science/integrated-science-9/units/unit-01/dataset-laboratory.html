Unit 01 · Required Dataset Laboratory

Urban Shade & Surface Temperature

Analyze a fictional instructional dataset, describe association and uncertainty, and avoid claiming causation beyond the study design.

No Physical MaterialsScatter PlotCorrelation ≠ Causation

Study description

At eight fictional paved sites, a student estimated the percentage of the measurement area shaded at 2:00 p.m. and recorded surface temperature with the same infrared thermometer. The student did not assign shade levels and did not control every site difference.

Tasks

  1. Create a scatter plot with shade percentage on the x-axis and temperature on the y-axis.
  2. Describe the overall association.
  3. Identify the site that does not fit the decreasing pattern as closely as expected.
  4. Calculate the temperature difference between Sites A and G.
  5. Write one supported claim.
  6. Write one causal claim that the observational design does not establish.
  7. Identify at least three possible confounding variables.
  8. Propose a controlled follow-up investigation.

Interpretation boundary

The dataset supports a negative association in these fictional observations: sites with more estimated shade generally had lower measured surface temperatures. It does not, by itself, prove that shade was the only cause because surface material, moisture, wind, prior exposure, thermometer angle, and other site differences were not fully controlled.
