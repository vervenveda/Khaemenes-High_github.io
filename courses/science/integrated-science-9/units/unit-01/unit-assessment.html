Constructed Response 1 · Investigation Design

Design an investigation of how drop height affects the mean bounce height of the same ball on the same surface. Include a testable question, directional hypothesis, independent and dependent variables, operational definitions, at least five controls, five trials per height, safety, raw-data table headings, graph choice, and two limitations.

Constructed Response 2 · Dataset Reasoning

A pendulum study reports mean periods of 0.90 s at 20 cm, 1.10 s at 30 cm, 1.28 s at 40 cm, 1.43 s at 50 cm, and 1.55 s at 60 cm. Write a CER explanation of the relationship in this study. State one limitation and one specific improvement. Do not claim that every possible pendulum will follow exactly these values.

Constructed responses are scored with the Unit 01 rubric and are not automatically graded by the browser.
