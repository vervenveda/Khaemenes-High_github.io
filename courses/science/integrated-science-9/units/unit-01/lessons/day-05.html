Unit 01 · Day 5

Plan the Paper Helicopter

Design a safe paper-helicopter investigation before collecting any results.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Normal scissors and clear drop areaPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Select one independent variable
  • Write a repeatable method with controls and trials
  • Create a safety scan and data table

Materials

  • Plain paper
  • Metric ruler
  • Pencil
  • Scissors used normally and safely
  • Paper clips optional
  • Stopwatch
Tier 1 · Normal scissors and clear drop area

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop when the environment, equipment, supervision, or learner needs differ from the written activity.

1. Investigation question

Recommended Core question: How does rotor length affect the mean fall time of a paper helicopter dropped from a fixed height?

Foundation learners may compare two rotor lengths. Core learners should compare at least three. Extended learners may test five levels and examine whether the pattern is linear.

2. Design requirements

  • Use one paper template and change only rotor length.
  • Define drop height, release method, timing start and stop, and landing area.
  • Use at least five trials per rotor length.
  • Record every trial before calculating means.
  • Keep people, pets, fans, open windows, stairs, and fragile objects away from the drop zone.
Prediction

Write a directional hypothesis and explain the scientific idea behind it.

3. Approval gate

Do not begin data collection until materials, drop area, method, variable levels, trial count, table headings, and stop conditions are complete.
