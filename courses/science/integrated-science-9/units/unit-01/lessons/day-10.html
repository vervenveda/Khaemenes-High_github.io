Unit 01 · Day 10

Design Challenge & Mastery

Integrate the full inquiry process through an independent design task, mastery checks, correction, and reflection.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Design a safe and repeatable investigation
  • Complete mastery assessments and corrections
  • Reflect on evidence quality and next steps

Materials

  • Investigation design task
  • Calculator
  • Science notebook
  • Unit portfolio
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop when the environment, equipment, supervision, or learner needs differ from the written activity.

1. Investigation design challenge

Design a fair test of how paper type affects the mass of water absorbed by a fixed-size sheet. The task requires an operational definition of absorbency, controls, repeated trials, safety, data table, graph choice, and limitations.

Open Design Task

2. Mastery sequence

  1. Complete the 15-question mastery quiz.
  2. Review every explanation.
  3. Correct misconceptions and retake until at least 80% is reached.
  4. Complete the 24-question objective unit assessment.
  5. Complete both constructed responses using the rubric.
Open QuizOpen Assessment

3. Final reflection

Explain how your investigation design changed from the first question to the final evidence. Identify the strongest feature of your method and the most important remaining limitation.
