Unit 01 · Day 6

Conduct & Analyze

Collect transparent repeated measurements, calculate a mean and range, graph the result, and evaluate anomalies and limitations.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Clear drop areaPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Carry out a planned investigation consistently
  • Calculate mean and range
  • Evaluate anomalies, limitations, and improvements

Materials

  • Completed paper helicopters
  • Stopwatch
  • Metric ruler or tape
  • Science notebook
  • Calculator
Tier 1 · Clear drop area

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop when the environment, equipment, supervision, or learner needs differ from the written activity.

1. Collect raw data

Use the approved plan. Record every trial immediately. Do not repeat only the trials that disagree with the prediction.

Stop conditions: unsafe drop location, damaged cutting tool, uncontrolled airflow, people entering the landing zone, or any unplanned change that prevents a fair comparison.

2. Process the data

For each rotor length:

  1. Add the trial times.
  2. Divide by the number of trials to calculate the mean.
  3. Subtract the smallest value from the largest to calculate the range.
  4. Plot rotor length on the x-axis and mean fall time on the y-axis.
Example only: 1.4, 1.5, 1.5, 1.6, 1.5 seconds have a mean of 1.5 s and a range of 0.2 s.

3. Evaluate

  • Was the expected trend observed?
  • How large was variation within each condition?
  • Were any trials anomalous?
  • Which control was hardest to maintain?
  • What improvement would most strengthen the evidence?
