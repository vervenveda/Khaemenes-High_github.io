Unit 01 · Day 4

Measurement Quality

Select suitable tools and units, distinguish accuracy from precision, and record resolution, variation, and uncertainty honestly.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish accuracy, precision, and resolution
  • Select a suitable measuring tool and unit
  • Describe uncertainty without claiming false exactness

Materials

  • Metric ruler
  • Stopwatch or timer
  • Three safe objects
  • Science notebook
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop when the environment, equipment, supervision, or learner needs differ from the written activity.

1. Accuracy, precision, and resolution

  • Accuracy: closeness to a trusted reference value.
  • Precision: closeness among repeated measurements.
  • Resolution: the smallest change a tool displays or distinguishes.
  • Uncertainty: the reasonable doubt associated with a measured or calculated result.
Important: Repeated measurements can be very precise and still inaccurate if the tool is miscalibrated or the method has a systematic bias.

3. Short measurement audit

  1. Measure one object three times.
  2. Record all values and units.
  3. State the tool’s smallest marked or displayed interval.
  4. Calculate the range.
  5. Identify one random and one possible systematic source of error.
