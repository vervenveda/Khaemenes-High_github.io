Unit 07 · Real substance records

Structure–Property Evidence Laboratory

Compare actual substances representing ionic, simple molecular, giant covalent, layered covalent, and metallic structures.

Data caution

Phase-change values depend on pressure, purity, allotrope, and measurement method. Terms such as “very high” are used where ordinary melting is complicated by sublimation or phase change.

Evidence matrix

Compare at least four materials. For each, identify direct property evidence, the bonding/structure inference, and one competing or limiting factor.
