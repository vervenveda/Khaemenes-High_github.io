# Unit 05 Pacing Guide

Weeks 13–15, fifteen lessons.

- Week 1: ecological organization, food webs, energy, matter cycles, biodiversity survey
- Week 2: population sampling, growth, interactions, disturbance, conservation
- Week 3: observed selection, microevolution/speciation/macroevolution, equid fossils, human origins/worldviews, evidence hearing and mastery

Real observations and public datasets are preferred. Models and worldview sources must be labeled.
