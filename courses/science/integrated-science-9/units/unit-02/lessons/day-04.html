1. Major components

StructureMain contribution
NucleusEncloses most DNA
RibosomesBuild proteins

Golgi apparatusModifies, sorts, and packages
MitochondriaCarry out most aerobic respiration stages
ChloroplastsCapture light energy for photosynthesis
VacuolesStore substances; central vacuoles support turgor

2. Plant and animal cells

Plant cells have plasma membranes, mitochondria, ribosomes, and other eukaryotic structures. Many also have cellulose walls, a large central vacuole, and—in photosynthetic tissues—chloroplasts. Animal cells lack cellulose walls and chloroplasts.

Avoid absolutes: Root cells usually lack chloroplasts; plant cells still respire in mitochondria.

3. Organelles as a network

A secreted protein may be built on ribosomes, processed through ER and Golgi membranes, moved in vesicles, and released through the plasma membrane.

Portfolio evidence

Trace one protein from DNA instructions to release. Name at least five structures involved.
