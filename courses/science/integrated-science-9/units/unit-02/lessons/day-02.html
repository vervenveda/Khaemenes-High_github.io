Unit 02 · Day 2

Scale, Units & Microscopes

Use millimeters, micrometers, field of view, and magnification calculations to interpret microscope evidence.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Convert between millimeters and micrometers
  • Calculate image magnification or actual size
  • Distinguish magnification from resolution

Materials

  • Metric ruler
  • Calculator
  • Notebook
  • Optional microscope or digital images
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Scale

One millimeter equals 1000 micrometers. Many animal and plant cells are tens of micrometers across; many bacteria are a few micrometers. Exact sizes vary by species, cell type, and condition.

Conversion: 0.075 mm × 1000 = 75 µm.

2. Magnification

Magnification = image size ÷ actual size. Use the same units before dividing.

A 24 mm image represents a 60 µm cell. Convert 24 mm to 24,000 µm. Magnification = 24,000 ÷ 60 = ×400.
Magnification is not resolution. Enlarging a blurry image does not reveal new detail.

3. Field of view

Increasing objective magnification usually decreases the visible field. Center specimens at low power before switching higher.

Portfolio evidence

An image is 45 mm wide at ×750. Calculate actual width in millimeters and micrometers.
