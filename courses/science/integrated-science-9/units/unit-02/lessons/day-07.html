Diffusion & Concentration Gradients

Explain diffusion as net movement arising from random particle motion and investigate temperature as a rate factor.

Tier 1 · Warm tap water only; protect surfaces

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Net movement

Particles move randomly. When concentration differs, more particles tend to leave the higher-concentration region, producing net movement down the gradient.

At equilibrium: particles do not stop. Random movement continues, but there is no net concentration change.

2. Rate factors

  • Steeper gradient generally increases net rate.
  • Higher temperature generally increases particle motion.
  • Greater surface area can increase total exchange.
  • Greater diffusion distance generally slows exchange.
