Unit 02 · Day 5

Virtual Microscopy Laboratory

Classify six specimens, calculate actual size, and evaluate the strengths and limits of microscope evidence.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Use visual evidence to classify cell types
  • Calculate actual size
  • Separate observed features from unsupported assumptions

Materials

  • Virtual microscopy page
  • Calculator
  • Notebook
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Laboratory expectations

For every station, record observed structures, classification, calculation, and uncertainty. A diagram is a simplified representation, not a direct photograph.

2. Complete the stations

Examine bacterial, onion epidermal, cheek epithelial, leaf, magnification, and model-evaluation stations.

Open Virtual Microscopy

3. Evidence statement

Portfolio evidence

Choose one station. Write what was directly visible, what classification was inferred, what additional evidence would help, and one limitation.

Pathway choices
FoundationComplete four stations with support.
CoreComplete all six stations.
ExtendedCompare light and electron microscopy conceptually.
