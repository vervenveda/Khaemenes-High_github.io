Unit 02 · Day 14

Models, Images & Limitations

Build and evaluate a cell model that communicates structure and function while stating scale, motion, and simplification limits.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish model types
  • Evaluate strengths and limitations
  • Create a structure–function model

Materials

  • Cell model task
  • Paper, reusable materials, or digital tool
  • Notebook
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Model types

  • Diagram: emphasizes selected relationships.
  • Physical model: supports spatial reasoning but may distort scale.
  • Microscope image: records instrument-mediated evidence.
  • Simulation: represents changing rules or processes.

2. Common distortions

Organelles may be shown too large, too colorful, too evenly spaced, or motionless. Cutaways expose structures that would not all appear in one optical section.

Model audit

Choose a cell illustration. Identify three useful features and three differences from a living cell.

3. Performance task

Create a model of a bacterial, photosynthetic plant, nonphotosynthetic plant, animal, fungal, or protist cell.
