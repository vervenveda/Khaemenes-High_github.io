Unit 02 · Day 13

Photosynthesis & Respiration

Track matter and energy through photosynthesis and aerobic cellular respiration without suggesting energy is created.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • State word equations
  • Explain how the processes are linked
  • Distinguish matter cycling from energy transfer

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Matter-and-energy template
  • Colored arrows optional
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Photosynthesis

In photosynthetic cells, chloroplast processes use light energy to build energy-rich organic molecules from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen.

Word equation: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen, using light energy.

2. Aerobic cellular respiration

Cells transfer chemical energy from glucose into forms that support cellular work. Most aerobic stages occur in mitochondria.

Word equation: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + transferred chemical energy.

Plants respire too. Photosynthetic plant cells perform both processes.

3. Matter and energy

Atoms are rearranged and can cycle. Energy flows and transforms; it is not recycled in the same way as matter.

Portfolio evidence

Connect chloroplasts, mitochondria, carbon dioxide, water, glucose, oxygen, light, and cellular work. Label matter and energy arrows.
