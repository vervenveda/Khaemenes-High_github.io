The Plasma Membrane

Model the plasma membrane as a dynamic, selectively permeable boundary that regulates exchange and communication.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Explain selective permeability
  • Relate membrane structure to transport
  • Distinguish a boundary from a sealed wall

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Paper and colored pencils or digital tool
  • Tokens optional
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Dynamic boundary

The plasma membrane separates the cell from its surroundings while allowing regulated exchange. Its phospholipid bilayer contains proteins and other components that move and interact.

2. Why substances differ

Small nonpolar molecules cross the lipid region more readily than many ions or large polar molecules. Channels, carriers, and pumps provide selective routes.

Model caution: A door model helps explain selectivity but may falsely suggest all transport proteins work alike.
