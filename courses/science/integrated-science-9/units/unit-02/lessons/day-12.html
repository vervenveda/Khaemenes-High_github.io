Unit 02 · Day 12

Tissues, Organs & Systems

Trace how specialized cells cooperate through tissues, organs, and organ systems.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Sequence levels of organization
  • Explain why organs depend on multiple tissues
  • Model system interdependence

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Organization cards optional
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Use only the stated materials and conditions. Stop whenever equipment, supervision, accessibility, or the environment differs from the written plan.

1. Hierarchy

Organelle → cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism. Each level contains interacting components and is part of a larger system.

2. One organ, many tissues

The heart contains cardiac muscle, connective tissue, nervous tissue, blood vessels, and lining tissues. An organ is not simply a large mass of one cell type.

System map

Trace oxygen from the atmosphere to a muscle-cell mitochondrion. Include two organ systems, one tissue, and one cellular structure.

3. Interdependence

Specialization requires exchange and coordination. Body systems interact rather than operating as isolated machines.
