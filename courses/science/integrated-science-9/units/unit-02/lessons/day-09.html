Active & Bulk Transport

Compare diffusion and osmosis with energy-dependent transport and large-scale membrane movement.

1. Against a gradient

Active transport moves substances against a concentration or electrochemical gradient using cellular energy and specific proteins. It helps cells maintain internal conditions that differ from their surroundings.

2. Examples

  • Root cells can accumulate mineral ions from dilute soil water.
  • Animal cells maintain ion gradients used in nerve and muscle function.
  • Cells absorb some nutrients through coupled transport systems.
Precision: “Active” refers to energy-dependent transport, not physical movement of the whole cell.

3. Bulk movement

Endocytosis brings material into membrane-bound vesicles. Exocytosis releases material when vesicles fuse with the plasma membrane.

Portfolio evidence
