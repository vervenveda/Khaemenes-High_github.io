1. Osmosis

Osmosis is net water movement through a selectively permeable membrane from higher free-water concentration to lower free-water concentration. In simple dilute solutions, this is often from lower solute concentration toward higher solute concentration.

2. Cell responses

Animal cells can swell or shrink. Plant cells become turgid in sufficiently dilute surroundings and may plasmolyze in concentrated surroundings.

Do not confuse: the cell wall is porous; the plasma membrane provides selective control.

3. Dataset laboratory

Analyze potato-cylinder data, calculate percent mass changes, estimate zero change, and identify limitations.
