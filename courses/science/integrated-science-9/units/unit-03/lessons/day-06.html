Digestion & Absorption

Follow matter through ingestion, digestion, absorption, assimilation, and egestion.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Sequence digestive processes
  • Relate organs to function
  • Distinguish absorption from assimilation

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Digestive pathway
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Pathway

Mouth → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum → anus. Salivary glands, liver, gall bladder, and pancreas contribute.

Digestion

Physical digestion makes smaller pieces. Chemical digestion uses enzymes to make smaller soluble molecules. Emulsification increases fat surface area.

Absorption and assimilation

Villi and microvilli increase absorption area. Absorption moves nutrients into blood or lymph; assimilation is cellular uptake and use.

Trace starch to glucose used by a muscle cell.
