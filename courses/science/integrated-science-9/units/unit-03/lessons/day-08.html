Kidneys, Excretion & Water Balance

Model filtration, selective reabsorption, urine formation, urea removal, ions, and water conservation.

Materials

Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Excretion

Lungs excrete carbon dioxide. Kidneys remove urea and regulate water and ions. Egestion removes undigested digestive material.

Nephron

Small substances enter filtrate. Useful substances are selectively reabsorbed. Urine contains urea plus regulated water and ions.

Boundary: A nephron is more than a passive filter.

ADH

When body water is low, increased ADH promotes more water reabsorption and smaller-volume, more concentrated urine.

Build a dehydration negative-feedback loop.
