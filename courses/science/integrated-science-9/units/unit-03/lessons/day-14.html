Unit 03 · Day 14

Integrated Systems Case Study

Explain a whole-body response using transport, gas exchange, digestion, excretion, signals, temperature, and evidence limits.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Integrate at least five systems
  • Use data without overclaiming
  • State limitations and privacy safeguards

Materials

  • Case-study page
  • Notebook
  • Calculator
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Scenario

A fictional student walks briskly uphill on a warm day after lunch. Heart rate, breathing, sweating, thirst, and urine concentration can change.

Systems questions

How do respiratory, circulatory, digestive, liver, nervous, endocrine, skin, muscular, and urinary functions cooperate?
