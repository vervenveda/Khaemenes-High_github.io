Unit 03 · Day 10

Blood-Glucose Regulation

Model insulin, glucagon, liver storage, cellular uptake, meals, fasting, and activity.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Explain insulin and glucagon
  • Build negative-feedback models
  • Separate models from diagnosis

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Scenario cards
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

After a meal

Rising blood glucose stimulates insulin, promoting uptake and storage, including glycogen formation.

Between meals

Falling blood glucose stimulates glucagon, promoting liver release of glucose.

Antagonistic hormones: Opposing effects support regulation.

Evidence limits

Meals, activity, sleep, stress, illness, medicines, hormones, and individual biology affect real regulation.
