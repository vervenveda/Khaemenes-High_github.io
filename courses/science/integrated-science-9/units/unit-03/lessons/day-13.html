Unit 03 · Day 13

Defense, Immunity & Evidence

Distinguish barriers, innate response, specific immunity, memory, and trustworthy health claims.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Compare defense layers
  • Explain antigen specificity and memory
  • Evaluate health claims

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Evidence checklist
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Preventing entry

Skin, mucus, cilia, tears, stomach acid, and other barriers reduce pathogen entry.

Response after entry

Innate responses act broadly. Specific responses involve lymphocytes, antibodies, antigens, and memory cells.

Precision: Symptoms, inflammation, immune response, and pathogen presence are related but not interchangeable.

Evidence literacy

Check methods, comparison groups, sample size, uncertainty, funding, conflicts, and whether the claim exceeds evidence.

Rewrite “This single food prevents every infection” as a defensible statement.
