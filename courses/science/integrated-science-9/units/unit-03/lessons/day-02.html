Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Double circulation

The pulmonary circuit links heart and lungs. The systemic circuit links heart and body tissues. The circuits connect in sequence.

Direction matters

Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward it. Pulmonary vessels show why oxygen content does not define vessel type.

Route

Trace blood from a leg muscle capillary to an alveolus and back.

Transported materials

Blood transports gases, nutrients, water, ions, hormones, immune components, wastes, and heat.
