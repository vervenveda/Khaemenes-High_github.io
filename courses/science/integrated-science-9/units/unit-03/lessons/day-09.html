Thermoregulation

Explain heat production and transfer through skin, circulation, muscles, signals, and behavior.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Model temperature feedback
  • Explain heat-loss and heat-conservation responses
  • Distinguish temperature and thermal energy

Materials

  • Simulator
  • Notebook
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Heat transfer

Metabolism generates thermal energy. Radiation, convection, conduction, and evaporation transfer heat.

Warm and cold responses

Warm responses can include sweating and increased skin blood flow. Cold responses can include reduced skin blood flow and shivering. Behavior also matters.
