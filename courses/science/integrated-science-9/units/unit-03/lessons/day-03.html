Heart structure

Atria receive blood; ventricles pump it. Valves reduce backflow. The left ventricle has a thicker wall because systemic circulation requires greater pressure.

Vessels

VesselStructureRole
ArteryThick, elastic, muscularFlow away under pressure
VeinThinner, large lumen, valvesReturn toward heart
CapillaryVery thin exchange wallTissue exchange

Blood

Plasma transports dissolved substances and heat; red blood cells transport oxygen; white blood cells defend; platelets and clotting proteins limit blood loss.

Explain why an artery, capillary, and red blood cell have different structures.
