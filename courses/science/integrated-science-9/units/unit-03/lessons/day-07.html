The Liver & Nutrient Regulation

Connect absorbed nutrients, bile, storage, conversion, detoxification, and urea formation.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Explain selected liver functions
  • Trace nutrients from intestine to tissues
  • Use precise detoxification language

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Liver map
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Metabolic hub

Blood from the digestive tract carries absorbed nutrients to the liver, which stores, converts, releases, and processes substances.

Functions

  • Produces bile
  • Stores glycogen
  • Processes amino acids and forms urea
  • Processes many drugs and toxins
  • Produces plasma proteins
Precision: Detoxification does not make every exposure harmless.

Systems link

Explain how digestive, circulatory, endocrine, liver, and urinary functions cooperate after a protein-rich meal.
