Endocrine Coordination

Compare hormones in body fluids with targeted neural signaling and receptor specificity.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Define hormone and target cell
  • Compare endocrine and nervous signals
  • Explain integrated regulation

Materials

  • Notebook
  • Signal table
Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Hormonal communication

Endocrine glands release hormones into body fluids. Only cells with suitable receptors respond.

Comparison

FeatureNervousEndocrine
RouteNeurones and synapsesHormones through fluids
OnsetOften rapidOften slower
DurationOften briefOften longer

Cooperation

Regulation often includes both rapid neural signals and longer hormonal changes.
