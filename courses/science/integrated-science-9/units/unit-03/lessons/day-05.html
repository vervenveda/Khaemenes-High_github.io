Unit 03 · Day 5

Exercise-Response Evidence

Analyze response and recovery as examples of coordination, variation, and evidence limits.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Data-first; gentle movement optionalPortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

  • Calculate response and recovery
  • Relate changes to system demand
  • Avoid diagnosis from classroom data

Materials

  • Response laboratory
  • Calculator
  • Fictional dataset
Tier 1 · Data-first; gentle movement optional

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Safe participation

The dataset is the default. Optional gentle movement requires adult approval and suitable ability.

Stop for pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or concern.

Coordinated response

Activity can increase oxygen demand, carbon dioxide and heat production, heart rate, breathing rate, and skin blood flow.

Laboratory

Open Response Lab

Explain why one recovery curve cannot diagnose health or rank fitness.

Pathway choices
FoundationUse the fictional dataset.
CoreCollect optional low-intensity data.
ExtendedCompare absolute, percent, and slope measures.
