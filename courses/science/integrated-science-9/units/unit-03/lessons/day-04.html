Gas Exchange & Ventilation

Connect ventilation, alveolar diffusion, blood transport, tissue exchange, and cellular respiration.

45–60 minutesTier 1 · Home-safePortfolio Evidence

Learning objectives

Tier 1 · Home-safe

Activities are educational, not medical evaluation. Use provided datasets whenever personal measurement, movement, health history, or supervision makes direct participation inappropriate.

Privacy

Students never need to publish personal health data. Use anonymous labels or the fictional datasets.

Three processes

Ventilation moves air. Gas exchange moves gases across surfaces. Cellular respiration transfers chemical energy in cells.

Alveoli

Many alveoli create large area. Thin moist surfaces, ventilation, and capillary flow maintain diffusion gradients.

Precision: Molecules move both directions; net movement depends on the gradient.

Molecular pathway

Trace oxygen from inhaled air to a muscle mitochondrion and carbon dioxide back to exhaled air.
