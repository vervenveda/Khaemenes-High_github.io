# Health Information Evidence Checklist

- [ ] Authors and source are identifiable.
- [ ] Methods and comparison groups are described.
- [ ] Sample size and population are reported.
- [ ] Absolute numbers are provided when relevant.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limitations are stated.
- [ ] Funding and conflicts are disclosed.
- [ ] The claim matches the evidence.
- [ ] Correlation is not automatically called causation.
- [ ] General information is not used to diagnose an individual.
- [ ] Personal data are protected.
