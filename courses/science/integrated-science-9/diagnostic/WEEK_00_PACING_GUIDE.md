# Unit 00 Pacing Guide

## Week 1 — Readiness, Laboratory Safety, and Scientific Habits

| Day | Focus | Primary evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Science as a Way of Knowing | Observation/inference record and model critique |
| 2 | Risk, Safety, and Ethics | Risk scan, safety agreement, and safety verification |
| 3 | Measurement and SI Units | Repeated-measurement table and notebook entry |
| 4 | Evidence, Data, and Models | Evidence statement, source check, and model revision |
| 5 | Readiness and Learning Goals | Readiness report, portfolio setup, and inquiry goal |

## Completion requirements

- Five daily lessons marked complete
- Readiness diagnostic completed
- Safety verification passed at 15/15
- Safety agreement recorded
- Science portfolio setup recorded

The readiness score is not a permanent grade. It is used to select support and may be replaced by later evidence of mastery.
