  - https://www.act.org/content/act/en/college-and-career-readiness/standards.html

  - https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-english-language-and-composition

## Course guarantee and limitation

The package is designed to exceed commonly used Grade 9 expectations by combining complete Grade 9–10 ELA coverage with advanced rhetoric, research, media literacy, publication, and oral defense. Standards and laws change. Local schools and homeschool authorities remain responsible for attaching the current state-specific code crosswalk required for official reporting.

## Additional competencies

1. Research provenance and lateral reading
2. Ethical citation and rights awareness
3. Multilingual and translation awareness
4. Visual and multimodal rhetoric
5. Authentic publication and editorial workflow
6. Portfolio curation and oral defense
