12th Grade Language Arts

Advanced Universal Senior Literacy & Capstone

A rigorous, globally inclusive English IV program combining high-complexity independent reading, world literature, poetry and drama, rhetoric, argument, advanced research, media and quantitative literacy, college and workplace writing, accessible digital publication, a senior capstone, oral defense, and a graduation portfolio.
