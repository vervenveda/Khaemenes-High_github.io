# High School Home Integration

The current High School home page presents **Language & Literature** as a non-clickable `<article>`. Replace that card with the linked version below after uploading this package:

```html
<a class="card department-card" href="courses/language-arts/">
  <span class="icon">✒</span>
  <h3>Language & Literature</h3>
  <p>English 9–12, composition, literature, rhetoric, research, journalism, public speaking, and creative writing.</p>
</a>
```

Recommended Grade 9 featured-course card:

```html
<div class="featured-course">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">Grade 9 National Language Arts</p>

  </div>
  <a class="button primary" href="courses/language-arts/english-9/">Open English 9</a>
```
