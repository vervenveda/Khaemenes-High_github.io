One Course · Three Supports

Rigor without a single narrow doorway.

Foundation

Read-aloud support, chunked texts, vocabulary previews, guided annotation, paragraph frames, extended time, and additional models.

Core

The complete 36-week sequence, all major writings, seminars, research tasks, assessments, independent reading, and portfolio evidence.

Extended

Additional primary sources, comparative criticism, independent research, longer writing, publication leadership, and college-level rhetorical analysis.
