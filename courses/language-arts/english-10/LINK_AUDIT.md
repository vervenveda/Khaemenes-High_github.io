# English 10 Link Audit

## Internal route families

- Course home: `index.html`
- Units: `units/unit-01/` through `units/unit-12/`
- Weeks: `weeks/week-01/` through `weeks/week-36/`
- Assessments: diagnostic, 12 unit assessments, midterm, final
- Records: index, calendar, attendance, reading, gradebook, certificate
- Rubrics: index, literary analysis, argument, research, speaking, narrative
- Portfolio: `portfolio/index.html`
- Grade alias: `grades/grade-10/index.html`
- Supporting pages: reading list, standards, teacher guide, curriculum JSON

## External links

External links appear only on the standards page for official education sources. They open in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`.

1. Open course home.
2. Navigate to every unit.
3. Open Week 1, Week 18, Week 36.

6. Enter one project score.
