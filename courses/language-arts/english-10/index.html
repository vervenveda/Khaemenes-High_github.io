Twelve Three-Week Units

A deliberate 36-week progression.

Each unit contains three complete weeks, a major product, an interactive unit assessment, standards alignment, and portfolio evidence.

Term I · Weeks 1–3

Unit 01 · Foundations of Scholarly Reading & Writing

Establish scholar habits, diagnostic baselines, annotation, inference, paraphrase, summary, academic integrity, and assessment readiness.

Diagnostic portfolio: annotated reading, analytical paragraph, revised writing sample, and reflection.
Open Unit

Term I · Weeks 4–6

Unit 02 · Coming-of-Age & Literary Layers

Analyze literary elements, coming-of-age experiences, conflicts, point of view, and competing perspectives.

Character and perspective analysis with evidence map and discussion reflection.
Open Unit

Term I · Weeks 7–9

Unit 03 · Theme, Voice & Literary Analysis

Compare universal themes, examine author style, and complete a formal literary analysis essay through drafting and revision.

Multi-paragraph literary analysis essay and revision memo.
Open Unit

Term II · Weeks 10–12

Unit 04 · American Speeches, Essays & Rhetoric

Study historical American speeches and essays for structure, central idea, purpose, audience, appeals, devices, and rhetorical effectiveness.

Rhetorical analysis essay on a historical American speech or essay.
Open Unit

Term II · Weeks 13–15

Unit 05 · Argument, Debate & Civic Discourse

Compare opposing arguments, evaluate validity, write claims and counterclaims, and participate in structured discussion and debate.

Evidence-based argument essay with oral defense or formal debate.
Open Unit

Term II · Weeks 16–18

Unit 06 · Synthesis & Midyear Mastery

Synthesize information, refine grammar and style, organize portfolio evidence, and complete the cumulative midterm.

Source synthesis project, writing portfolio conference, and cumulative midterm examination.
Open Unit

Term III · Weeks 19–21

Unit 07 · Poetry, Ambiguity & Sound

Analyze poetic speaker, ambiguity, connotation, figurative language, mood, structure, sound, and comparison.

Comparative poetry analysis essay and expressive reading.
Open Unit

Term III · Weeks 22–24

Unit 08 · Classical, Mythic & Religious Adaptation

Read mythical, classical, and religious source texts as literature, then compare adaptations across texts and media.

Comparative adaptation essay or multimedia analysis.
Open Unit

Term III · Weeks 25–27

Unit 09 · Research Foundations

Design research questions, evaluate reliability and bias, take accurate notes, paraphrase, cite, and synthesize.

Research proposal, annotated bibliography, source evaluation matrix, and synthesis paragraph.
Open Unit

Term IV · Weeks 28–30

Unit 10 · Research Writing & Digital Publication

Write expository and argumentative research products, integrate sources, revise, publish digitally, and present findings.

Research paper, publication-ready digital product, and formal presentation.
Open Unit

Term IV · Weeks 31–33

Unit 11 · Drama, Shakespeare & Performance

Study dramatic structure, stagecraft, irony, Shakespearean language, rhetoric, interpretation, and performance.

Dramatic analysis and staged or recorded performance with reflection.
Open Unit

Term IV · Weeks 34–36

Unit 12 · Novel Study, Final Portfolio & Examination

Complete a long-form novel study, synthesize themes and perspectives, curate the final portfolio, and complete the final examination.

Novel analysis, student-led portfolio conference, cumulative final examination, and completion certificate.
Open Unit
