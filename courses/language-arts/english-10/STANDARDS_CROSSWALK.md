# English 10 Standards Crosswalk

The course uses Florida Grade 10 B.E.S.T. benchmark codes in the weekly and unit records and maintains nationally recognizable Grade 9–10 ELA/Literacy skill bands.

## Major domains

- Literary elements, layers, theme, perspective, coming-of-age, poetry, and adaptation
- Informational structure, central idea, historical speeches and essays
- Rhetoric, purpose, audience, appeals, opposing arguments, and validity
- Expository and argumentative writing
- Research, paraphrase, source evaluation, citation, and synthesis
- Speaking, listening, discussion, performance, and digital presentation
- Grammar, punctuation, vocabulary, etymology, connotation, voice, and tone
- Revision, publication, portfolio, and reflection

The complete code-to-week matrix is available in `standards.html` and `curriculum.json`.
