Four-Year Direction

Grade 9 establishes global and comparative foundations. Grade 10 develops United States history and historical reasoning. Grade 11 applies constitutional government, law, economics, financial literacy, media verification, and public policy. Grade 12 can advance into comparative politics, international relations, ethics, law, public leadership, and a senior research practicum without duplicating these sequences.
