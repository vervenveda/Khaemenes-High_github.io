Government, Law & Economics Honors

Grade 11 · ½ Government + ½ Economics · United States Government, Constitutional Law, Economics, Public Policy, Civic Literacy, and Media Analysis

An advanced 36-week inquiry course that moves from constitutional foundations and institutions through civil liberties, elections, media, economic reasoning, markets, macroeconomics, public finance, international trade, and a nonpartisan public-policy capstone.

Credit note: Transcript structure: one-half credit United States Government Honors (Weeks 1–18) and one-half credit Economics Honors (Weeks 19–36). Personal Financial Literacy is provided as a separate one-half-credit companion course.
