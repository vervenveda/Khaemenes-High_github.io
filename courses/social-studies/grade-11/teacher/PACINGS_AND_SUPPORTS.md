# Pacing and Supports

The default schedule is five lessons per week for 36 weeks. Block schedules may combine Monday/Tuesday and Wednesday/Thursday while preserving Friday assessment and correction. Do not compress source evaluation, calculation, writing, or reassessment into passive reading.

Supports: vocabulary preview, chunked readings, audio access, extended time, exemplars, graphic organizers, calculation templates, oral rehearsal, and targeted reteaching. Extensions: additional cases, sensitivity analysis, opposing briefs, original data collection, and public presentation.
