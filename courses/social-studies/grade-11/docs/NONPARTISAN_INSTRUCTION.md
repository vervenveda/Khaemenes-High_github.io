# Nonpartisan Instruction and Academic Independence

The course does not require loyalty to a party, candidate, ideology, business, advocacy organization, or course author. Political, constitutional, economic, and financial disagreements should be represented accurately and evaluated with consistent standards.

Students are graded on accuracy, evidence, authority, calculation, reasoning, counterargument, tradeoff analysis, citation, communication, and revision. Campaign or advocacy pages may be analyzed as primary-source political communication but may not be treated as neutral authority or required belief.
