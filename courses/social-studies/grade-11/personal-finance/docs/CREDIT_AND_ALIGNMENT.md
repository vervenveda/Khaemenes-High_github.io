# Credit and Alignment

This course is designed as a separate 0.5-credit Personal Financial Literacy and Money Management course. It should appear separately on the transcript.

Florida currently requires separate one-half credits in United States Government, Economics, and—depending on cohort—Personal Financial Literacy. The package keeps those records distinct. Course acceptance remains subject to local homeschool law, district policy, state requirements, college admissions rules, scholarship rules, and accreditor review. No local curriculum can guarantee universal acceptance.

The package overlays Florida standards, the NCSS C3 inquiry framework, grades 11–12 disciplinary literacy, national economics or personal-finance standards, and AP-style analytical skills. It does not claim College Board AP authorization or official state-exam status.
