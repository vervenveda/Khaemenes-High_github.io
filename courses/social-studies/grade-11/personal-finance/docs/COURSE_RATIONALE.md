# Course Rationale

The separate financial-literacy companion gives students practical decision-making and consumer-protection competence while preserving a transcript record distinct from economics.

The course emphasizes transferable inquiry: identify the correct institution or decision context, locate authoritative evidence, model tradeoffs, distinguish legal or factual claims from preferences, communicate a defensible conclusion, and revise after correction. Honors rigor comes from sustained reading, source corroboration, calculation and data interpretation, argumentation, cumulative assessment, and oral defense—not from merely increasing workload.
