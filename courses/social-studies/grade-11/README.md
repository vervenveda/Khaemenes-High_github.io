# Grade 11 Government, Law & Economics Honors

**Course code:** KHAE-SS11-GLE  
**Credit:** 1  
**Length:** 36 weeks · 180 daily lessons  
**Authors:** Jennifer Pearl and Autumn Pearl  
**Release:** 2026-08-05

The separate Personal Financial Literacy companion is stored under `personal-finance/`.
