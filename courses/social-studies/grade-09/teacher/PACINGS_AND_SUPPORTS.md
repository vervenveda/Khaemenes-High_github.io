# Pacing, Differentiation, and Support Guide

## Standard 55-minute lesson
- 5–8 minutes: retrieval warm-up
- 10–15 minutes: direct instruction or modeled thinking
- 15–20 minutes: guided source, map, or data practice
- 15–20 minutes: independent application
- 3–5 minutes: exit check

## Supported pathway
- Use the four reading headings as chunks.
- Preview six vocabulary terms before the reading.
- Provide audio read-aloud and sentence frames.
- Allow oral rehearsal before writing.
- Offer a partially labeled map or timeline.
- Reduce copying without reducing reasoning.
- Grade one paragraph for evidence and reasoning before requiring a longer response.

## Honors pathway
- Add a second primary source with a conflicting perspective.
- Require source citation and a counterclaim.
- Ask students to challenge the course periodization.
- Add an original data visualization.
- Connect the week to a previous region or a present policy problem.

## English learner supports
- Permit bilingual glossary development.
- Explicitly teach transition words for cause, comparison, and qualification.
- Pair visuals with concise definitions.
- Accept oral or recorded first drafts when appropriate.

## Accessibility
- All core readings are available in HTML and support browser text-to-speech.
- Print styles remove navigation and preserve readable contrast.
- Buttons and inputs include visible focus states.
- Teachers may provide extended time, reduced-distraction settings, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, or alternate response formats according to student need.

## Weekly planning checklist
1. Review sensitive-content notes.
2. Print or assign the source, map, and data materials.
3. Select standard, supported, or honors pathway.
4. Verify external links before assigning them; the original reading remains sufficient if a link is unavailable.
5. Prepare the quiz and retake plan.
6. Export a backup after grading.
