# Grade 9 Global Studies Honors — Teacher Handbook

## Course purpose
This 36-week course integrates world history, geography, civics, economics, disciplinary literacy, media literacy, and informed civic action. It contains 180 daily lessons, 108 principal assignments, 36 weekly quizzes, a midterm, a cumulative final, and a civic capstone.

## Weekly teaching rhythm
- **Monday:** chronology, geography, background knowledge, vocabulary, and inquiry framing.
- **Tuesday:** original weekly reading and a contextualized primary-source investigation.
- **Wednesday:** map, graph, budget, timeline, demographic, or economic data.
- **Thursday:** seminar, debate, simulation, research, or evidence-based writing.
- **Friday:** retrieval practice, synthesis, weekly quiz, corrections, and portfolio update.

## Assessment philosophy
Objective items use definitive answers and explanations. Written work may support more than one conclusion when the evidence and reasoning are sound. Score the quality of evidence, accuracy, reasoning, and communication rather than political agreement.

## Suggested grading
- Three weekly assignments: 45%
- Weekly quizzes: 20%
- Midterm: 12%
- Final: 18%
- Journal, discussion, reflection: 5%

## Retakes and corrections
Permit a retake after the student:
1. Revisits the relevant reading or lesson.
2. Completes an error-analysis form.
3. Explains why the original answer was incorrect.
4. Provides the evidence supporting the correction.

The portal preserves the best objective score and the number of attempts.

## Sensitive content
War, slavery, genocide, religious conflict, persecution, and colonial violence must be taught precisely and without spectacle. Do not use simulations that ask students to pretend to be enslaved people, Holocaust victims, perpetrators, refugees, or targets of violence. Use testimony, law, artifacts, maps, and carefully contextualized evidence.

## Religious studies
Teach religions and philosophies academically. Describe beliefs accurately, compare traditions without ranking them, avoid devotional requirements, and include internal diversity.

## Media and AI literacy
Students must verify claims, images, statistics, quotations, and citations against original or authoritative sources. Generative AI may support brainstorming or feedback only when teacher-permitted. Invented evidence or citations receive no credit.

## Homeschool and local compliance
This packet is a curriculum and recordkeeping tool, not an automatic guarantee of state, district, scholarship, umbrella-school, or accrediting approval. Verify local course titles, required instruction, assessment, attendance, portfolio, and transcript rules.
