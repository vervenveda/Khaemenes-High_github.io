# Grade 9 Global Studies Honors — Rubrics

## Universal 20- or 30-point assignment rubric
Score each dimension on a four-level scale.

| Dimension | 4 — Advanced | 3 — Proficient | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Multiple accurate, relevant details; sources identified precisely | Sufficient accurate evidence; sources generally clear | Limited, partly relevant, or weakly identified evidence | Unsupported claim or inaccurate evidence |
| Reasoning | Explains relationships, complexity, and limitations | Clearly explains how evidence supports the claim | Some explanation, but reasoning is incomplete | Summary, assertion, or reasoning does not connect |
| Disciplinary accuracy | Uses chronology, geography, economics, civics, and historical concepts correctly | Minor errors do not undermine understanding | Several errors or vague concepts | Major misconceptions |
| Communication | Purposeful organization, precise language, and effective citation | Clear organization and readable academic language | Uneven organization or clarity | Difficult to follow or incomplete |

## Constructed response rubric — 5 points
- 1 point: Direct, defensible claim
- 2 points: Two accurate pieces of evidence
- 1 point: Explanation connecting evidence to the claim
- 1 point: Complexity, qualification, limitation, or clear communication

## Short-answer examination rubric — 10 points
- 2: Accurate claim
- 4: Specific relevant evidence
- 3: Reasoning and relationship
- 1: Clarity and organization

## Evidence-based essay rubric — 30 points
- Thesis: 4
- Contextualization: 4
- Evidence: 10
- Historical reasoning: 8
- Organization and citation: 4

## Civic capstone rubric — 100 points
- Public problem and compelling question: 10
- Historical and geographic context: 15
- Source quality and verification: 15
- Stakeholder and governing-authority analysis: 10
- Data and economic tradeoffs: 15
- Feasible options and recommendation: 15
- Lawful civic action step: 10
- Presentation, reflection, and citation: 10
