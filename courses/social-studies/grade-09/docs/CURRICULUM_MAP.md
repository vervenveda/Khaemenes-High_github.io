## Required weekly outputs
Every week contains five daily lessons, one original reading, vocabulary, one source study, one map/data investigation, three assignments, one 10-question objective quiz, one constructed response, support options, honors extensions, and teacher answer guidance.

## Major assessments
- Week 18: 45-question midterm, three short responses, and one evidence-based essay.
- Week 36: 55-question cumulative final, three short responses, and one evidence-based essay.
- Weeks 34–36: nonpartisan civic-action capstone.
