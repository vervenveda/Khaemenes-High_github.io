# Assessment Plan

## Weekly
Each week includes:
- 10 objective questions worth 2 points each
- 1 constructed response worth 5 points
- Answer explanations for all objective questions
- Retake and correction support

## Midterm
Coverage: Weeks 1–17
- 45 objective questions
- 3 short responses
- 1 evidence-based essay
- Recommended time: 105 minutes

## Final
Coverage: Weeks 1–36, with substantial material from the second semester
- 55 objective questions
- 3 short responses
- 1 evidence-based essay
- Recommended time: 120 minutes

## Weighting
- Assignments 45%
- Weekly quizzes 20%
- Midterm 12%
- Final 18%
- Journal/discussion/reflection 5%

Objective scores are calculated in the browser. Teachers score written work using the included rubrics.
