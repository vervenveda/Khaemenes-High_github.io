Assignment Guidance

1. Source Lab — Art as Political and Social Evidence

Teacher note: Accept multiple interpretations when claims are supported. Do not award full evidence credit for summary without analysis.

Sample-answer guidance: A strong response will identify the source’s origin and audience, use at least two specific details, and explain how the source contributes to the question “How did renewed study, urban wealth, artistic experimentation, and printing reshape authority and knowledge?” while naming one limitation.

2. Atlas and Data Investigation

Teacher note: Check labels, legend, scale or units, and whether the student distinguishes observation from explanation.

Sample-answer guidance: A strong product uses accurate labels and units, identifies a meaningful pattern, proposes an evidence-based explanation, and notes what the visualization cannot prove.

3. Evidence-Based Seminar or Argument

Teacher note: Score the quality of reasoning rather than the political or moral position selected.

Sample-answer guidance: Answers may reasonably take different positions. Full-credit work directly answers “Did printing weaken authority, strengthen authority, or do both?,” uses accurate evidence from at least two source types, and addresses a serious counterargument.
