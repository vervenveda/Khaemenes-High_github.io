# Course Rationale

Grades 9–11 establish global history and geography, United States history, constitutional government, economics, and personal financial literacy. Grade 12 therefore functions as a senior synthesis rather than another survey.

The course asks students to apply prior knowledge through comparative politics, international relations, international law, human rights, migration, development, global political economy, environment, conflict, security, technology, public health, media verification, and independent research.

The program deliberately combines:
- AP Comparative Government–level concepts and analytical skills;
- IB Global Politics–level international breadth as a benchmark;
- AP Research–level sustained inquiry, paper, presentation, and oral defense as a benchmark;
- NCSS C3 inquiry;
- grades 11–12 disciplinary literacy;
- mandatory correction and demonstrated mastery.

The title remains **Honors**. It must not be represented as AP or IB unless the appropriate external authorization is obtained.
