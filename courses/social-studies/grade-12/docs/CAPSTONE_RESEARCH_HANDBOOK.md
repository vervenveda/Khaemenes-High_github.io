# Senior Research Capstone Handbook

## Required final products
1. 4,000–5,000-word academic thesis.
2. Executive summary.
3. Annotated bibliography and source-evaluation record.
4. Methods and ethics statement.
5. Evidence or data appendix where appropriate.
6. AI-assistance disclosure.
7. Public communication or civic product.
8. Presentation.
9. Oral defense.
10. Revision memo and final portfolio.

## Required paper architecture
- Title and abstract/executive summary
- Research question and significance
- Scope and definitions
- Literature and evidence review
- Methodology and ethics
- Findings
- Analysis and competing explanations
- Limitations
- Implications or recommendations
- Conclusion
- References
- Appendices and disclosure

## Ten checkpoints

### CP01 · Week 2 · Integrity Agreement and Research Interests
Sign the integrity and AI-disclosure agreement; identify three feasible issue areas.

### CP02 · Week 6 · Preliminary Question and Scope
Submit a focused question, cases, time period, concepts, and exclusions.

### CP03 · Week 10 · Source Map and Annotated Bibliography I
Submit at least eight credible sources, including primary or authoritative records.

### CP04 · Week 14 · Comparative Framework and Feasibility Review
Defend case selection, variables, access to evidence, ethics, and schedule.

### CP05 · Week 18 · Formal Proposal Defense
Pass the proposal defense and complete all required revisions.

### CP06 · Week 22 · Methods and Evidence Collection Audit
Submit methods, evidence log, source evaluation, and data-management plan.

### CP07 · Week 26 · Annotated Bibliography II and Preliminary Analysis
Show a mature evidence base, coding or calculations, and competing explanations.

### CP08 · Week 30 · Findings Conference and Integrity Audit
Present preliminary findings, negative evidence, limitations, and disclosure record.

### CP09 · Week 34 · Complete Draft and Revision Memo
Submit the full paper, peer review, citation audit, and revision plan.

### CP10 · Week 36 · Final Thesis, Defense, and Portfolio Audit
Submit final artifacts and pass the oral defense and graduation audit.

## Oral defense questions
The evaluator should ask questions such as:
- Why is this question important and answerable?
- Why did you select these cases, measures, or sources?
- What is the strongest evidence against your conclusion?
- Which source was most limited and how did you compensate?
- What ethical risk did you identify?
- What result surprised you?
- What evidence would make you revise your conclusion?
- Which part of the work is independently yours?
- How did any automated assistance affect the process?
- What should a responsible audience do with the conclusion?

## Approval rule
A checkpoint may be approved, conditionally approved with documented revisions, or returned. The final credit audit requires all ten checkpoints to be approved.
