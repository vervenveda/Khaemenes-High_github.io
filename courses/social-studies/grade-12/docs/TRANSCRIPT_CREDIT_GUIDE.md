# Transcript Credit Guide

## Recommended record
**KHAE-SS12-GPIR — Global Politics, International Relations & Senior Research Honors — 1.0 credit**

Record the final percentage and honors designation only after the Teacher Portal shows every mandatory gate passed and the evaluator issues credit.

## Do not record as
- AP Comparative Government;
- AP Research;
- IB Global Politics;
- nationally accredited;
- accepted by every state.

Those designations require separate authorization or review.

## Retain
- final report;
- attendance and hours;
- gradebook;
- curriculum map;
- standards crosswalk;
- thesis;
- defense rubric;
- major assessment scores;
- integrity verification;
- evaluator signature;
- completion certificate.

## Transfer note
When transferring the course, provide the descriptive transcript profile and supporting portfolio. The receiving authority decides equivalency.
