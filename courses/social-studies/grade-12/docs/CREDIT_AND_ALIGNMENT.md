# Credit and Alignment

## Credit profile
- Course: Global Politics, International Relations & Senior Research Honors
- Local code: `KHAE-SS12-GPIR`
- Grade: 12
- Level: Honors
- Length: 36 weeks
- Credit: 1.0
- Minimum documented time: 150 hours
- Planned lessons: 180
- Principal assignments: 108
- Weekly mastery quizzes: 36

## Alignment framework
The course is crosswalked to:
- NCSS College, Career, and Civic Life (C3) Framework;
- grades 11–12 reading and writing in history/social studies;
- AP Comparative Government concepts and skills as a rigor benchmark;
- AP Research inquiry, paper, presentation, and defense as a rigor benchmark;
- IB Global Politics themes as a breadth benchmark;
- NCSS thematic strands;
- advanced civic, economic, geographic, historical, legal, media, quantitative, and research literacy.

## Portability limitation
The United States has no single federal high-school social-studies graduation course. The responsible authority must review current state, district, school, umbrella-school, scholarship, and admissions requirements before awarding or transferring credit.
