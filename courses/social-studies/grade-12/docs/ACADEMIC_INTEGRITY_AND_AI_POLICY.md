# Academic Integrity and AI Policy

## Nonnegotiable integrity rules
The following invalidate affected work until corrected:
- plagiarism;
- fabricated or altered evidence;
- invented sources, quotations, interviews, or data;
- falsified citations or attendance;
- undisclosed ghostwriting;
- submitting automated output without verification and disclosure;
- misrepresenting group work as independent work.

## Permitted support
With teacher approval, students may use tools for:
- brainstorming research questions;
- accessibility and language support;
- outlining;
- grammar and clarity feedback;
- coding or spreadsheet troubleshooting;
- citation formatting;
- practice questioning.

## Required disclosure
The final product must disclose material assistance, including:
- tool or person;
- date;
- purpose;
- what material was accepted, changed, or rejected;
- how factual claims and citations were independently verified.

## Student responsibility
Students remain responsible for:
- every factual claim;
- every citation;
- every calculation;
- all representations of method and evidence;
- privacy and consent;
- the ability to defend the work orally.

## Evaluator verification
The evaluator should inspect drafts, research logs, citations, version history, disclosure statements, and oral-defense performance before verifying integrity.
