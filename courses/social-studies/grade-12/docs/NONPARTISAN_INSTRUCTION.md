# Nonpartisan and Viewpoint-Neutral Instruction

Students are not graded on party, candidate, ideology, religion, nationality, or policy preference.

They are graded on:
- factual and legal accuracy;
- source authority;
- evidence;
- comparative method;
- quantitative reasoning;
- argument and counterargument;
- citation;
- integrity;
- revision;
- oral defense.

Required civic activities must offer neutral alternatives. A student may observe a public meeting, analyze a public record, conduct nonpartisan voter education, map community resources, provide public service, submit evidence-based public comment, interview stakeholders with consent, or create an educational product. No student is required to campaign, donate, endorse, protest, lobby for a prescribed position, or disclose private political beliefs.

Campaign or candidate materials may be analyzed as political communication, but they are not treated as neutral academic authority.
