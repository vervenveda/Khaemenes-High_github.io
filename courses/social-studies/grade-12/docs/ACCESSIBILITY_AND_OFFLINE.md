# Accessibility and Offline Use

The portal includes:
- responsive layout;
- keyboard-accessible controls;
- light/dark themes;
- font scaling;
- text-to-speech where browser-supported;
- printable student packets and teacher guides;
- local browser records;
- downloadable JSON backups;
- a service-worker shell and offline fallback.

Accommodations may include:
- audio access;
- extended time;
- chunked source presentation;
- speech-to-text;
- graphic organizers;
- alternative accessible formats;
- additional practice and reassessment.

Accommodations should preserve the final accuracy, evidence, reasoning, integrity, and mastery standard.
