# Homeschool Evaluator Guide

## Evaluator role
The evaluator independently verifies that the student completed the documented course and demonstrated mastery. The portal passcode is only a convenience gate and is not secure authentication.

## Review sequence
1. Confirm student identity and dates of study.
2. Compare attendance and hours against lesson records.
3. Inspect a representative sample from every quarter.
4. Verify all 36 quiz mastery records and corrections.
5. Verify every major assessment and individual threshold.
6. Inspect all ten capstone checkpoints.
7. Read the thesis and source/disclosure record.
8. conduct or review the oral defense.
9. Verify the cumulative final.
10. Complete the integrity review.
11. Resolve discrepancies.
12. Issue final credit only after every gate passes.

## Suggested portfolio sample
Retain at least:
- four weekly packets per quarter;
- all major assessments;
- correction records;
- data and source laboratories;
- capstone proposal, drafts, and final;
- engagement activity;
- examinations;
- evaluator rubrics;
- attendance and final report.

## Jurisdictional review
The family remains responsible for current state notice, portfolio, evaluation, testing, umbrella-school, and diploma requirements.
