# Research Notes and Benchmark Sources

The course was designed using current official or authoritative benchmark materials. Review them periodically because standards, course descriptions, and legal requirements can change.

## Principal sources
- NCSS C3 Framework: https://www.socialstudies.org/standards/c3
- Common Core history/social-studies literacy, grades 11–12: https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RH/11-12/
- Common Core writing in history/social studies: https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/WHST/11-12/
- AP Comparative Government and Politics: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-comparative-government-and-politics
- AP Research: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-research
- IB Global Politics curriculum updates: https://www.ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/global-politics-updates/
- NCES SCED: https://nces.ed.gov/scedfinder/
- Education Commission of the States, graduation requirements: https://www.ecs.org/50-state-comparison-high-school-graduation-requirements-2023/

## Design rule
AP and IB materials are used as rigor and breadth benchmarks only. The Khaemenes course remains an honors course unless separately authorized.
