# Common Grade 12 Rubrics

## 100-point analytical performance
- Disciplinary accuracy and concept use: 20
- Authority and quality of evidence: 20
- Analysis, method, and causal/legal reasoning: 25
- Comparison, counterargument, limitations, and revision condition: 15
- Organization, citation, communication, and integrity: 20

## Weekly constructed response
- Precise claim: 2
- Accurate concepts: 2
- Two specific pieces of evidence: 2
- Reasoning: 2
- Counterargument or limitation: 1
- Revision condition: 1

## Reassessment
Correction is required before reassessment. Preserve both the attempt and the verified final result.
