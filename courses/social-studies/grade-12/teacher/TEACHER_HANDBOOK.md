# Grade 12 Teacher Handbook

## Course administration
- 36 weeks
- 1.0 honors credit
- 150 documented hours
- 180 lessons
- 108 principal assignments
- 36 weekly mastery quizzes
- 36 evaluator-approved weekly portfolios
- 13 mandatory major assessments
- 10 capstone checkpoints

## Weekly cycle
1. Concept and inquiry
2. Primary/authoritative source analysis
3. Comparative data or case analysis
4. Argument, seminar, or performance
5. Mastery quiz, correction, and capstone log

## Grading commitments
Grade evidence and reasoning, not political position. Return work containing unsupported claims, fabricated evidence, missing citation, or undisclosed material assistance.

## Graduation rule
Do not issue credit until the portal shows every mandatory gate passed. A high weighted average cannot replace the thesis, defense, final, engagement activity, hours, integrity review, or evaluator approval.
