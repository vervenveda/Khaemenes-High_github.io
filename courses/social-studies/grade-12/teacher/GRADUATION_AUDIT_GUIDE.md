# Graduation Audit Guide

1. Reconcile student identity, dates, attendance, and hours.
2. Confirm at least 162 daily lessons and all essential make-up work.
3. Confirm all 108 assignments are submitted.
4. Review and approve all 36 weekly portfolios.
5. Confirm all 36 weekly quiz best scores are at least 80%.
6. Enter every major-assessment score and verify submission.
7. Review the ten checkpoint artifacts and approve each.
8. Verify Semester 1, Semester 2, and overall minimums.
9. Inspect integrity and AI disclosures.
10. Verify evaluator review.
11. Issue credit only when the pre-issuance audit reports READY TO ISSUE CREDIT.
12. Print or export the final record and retain supporting evidence.
