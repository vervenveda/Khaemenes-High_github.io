# Pacing and Supports

The standard pace is one week per topic and approximately 250 minutes of direct weekly lessons, supplemented by reading, research, writing, revision, and capstone work. Document at least 150 total hours.

Supports may include audio, vocabulary preview, chunking, extended time, speech-to-text, graphic organizers, and additional practice. Do not reduce the final evidence, reasoning, integrity, or mastery standard.
