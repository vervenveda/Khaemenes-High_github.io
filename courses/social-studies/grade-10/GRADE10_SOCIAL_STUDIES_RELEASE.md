# Grade 10 Social Studies Release

**Release date:** August 5, 2026  
**Course:** Grade 10 United States History Honors  
**Code:** KHAE-SS10-USH  
**Status:** Complete curriculum package

## Included
- Full 36-week scope and sequence
- 180 daily lessons
- 108 assignments
- 36 weekly quizzes and answer keys
- Midterm and final examinations
- Civic capstone
- Teacher and student portals
- Printable weekly packets
- Progress reports and browser-local backups
- Accessibility and offline support
- Standards and required-instruction crosswalk

## Design intent
The course continues Grade 9 Global Studies Honors without duplicating its global-history sequence. It raises disciplinary literacy and historical reasoning toward advanced high-school expectations while remaining usable in Foundation, Core, and Extended pathways.
