United States History Honors

Grade 10 · History · Constitutional Government · Civic Literacy · Economics · Geography · Media Analysis

A rigorous 36-week inquiry course from Indigenous America and the founding era through Reconstruction, industrialization, world wars, civil rights, the Cold War, and contemporary constitutional challenges. Students evaluate evidence, interpret maps and data, write defensible arguments, and complete a nonpartisan civic capstone.

Credit note: This is a one-credit U.S. History Honors course with integrated constitutional and civic literacy. It does not claim to replace Florida’s separate one-half-credit United States Government requirement.
