# Course Rationale

Grade 9 Global Studies Honors already establishes global chronology, geography, comparative institutions, economics, media literacy, and inquiry. Grade 10 therefore advances into United States History rather than repeating world history.

The course uses a full-history framework from Indigenous America to the present, but its pacing gives greatest weight to Reconstruction through contemporary America. This preserves broad national coherence and aligns with Florida’s United States History course emphasis.

Integrated constitutional analysis prepares students for later United States Government and civic-literacy study. It does not replace the separate Government credit.

The instructional design is inquiry-centered and culturally inclusive without grading students for political agreement. Every disputed claim is evaluated through consistent evidence rules.
