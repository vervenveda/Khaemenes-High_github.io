# Research Notes

Research consulted current official and recognized sources available in August 2026:

- Florida Department of Education social-studies standards, graduation requirements, required instruction, and 2026–2027 Course Code Directory.
- CPALMS current United States History Honors course and benchmark associations.
- National Council for the Social Studies C3 Framework.
- Common Core literacy standards for History/Social Studies, Grades 9–10.
- College Board AP United States History course skills, themes, and chronology as an honors overlay.
- Cambridge IGCSE History evidence-analysis goals as an international overlay.

The course also uses official public-history resources from the Library of Congress, National Archives, Smithsonian, United States Holocaust Memorial Museum, Department of State Office of the Historian, Census Bureau, Federal Reserve History, EPA, Supreme Court, Congress, and Florida government.
