# Credit and Alignment

Recommended transcript title:

**United States History Honors — 1.0 credit**

This course is designed to support the Florida American History graduation requirement when used with appropriate instructional records and local review.

Do not label it as:
- AP United States History unless the course is authorized through the AP Course Audit.
- Cambridge IGCSE History credit or examination completion without an approved Cambridge centre and examination process.
- United States Government completion solely because government and constitutional literacy are integrated.
- Guaranteed acceptance in every jurisdiction.
