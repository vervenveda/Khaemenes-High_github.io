# Khaemenes High — Grade 10 United States History Honors

A complete 36-week, one-credit United States History Honors curriculum and browser-based student portal.

## Course totals
- 36 instructional weeks
- 180 daily lessons
- 108 principal assignments
- 36 weekly quizzes with retakes and corrections
- Midterm examination
- Cumulative final examination
- Nonpartisan civic capstone
- Teacher portal, answer keys, pacing guide, gradebook, attendance, and standards tracker
- Printable standalone student packets and teacher guides for every week
- Browser-local progress records, export/import, print reports, accessibility controls, and PWA/offline support

## Scope
The course begins with a concise foundation from Indigenous America through the Civil War and devotes the majority of the year to Reconstruction through the present. Constitutional government, economics, geography, media literacy, Florida history, and civic reasoning are integrated throughout.

## Credit statement
This package is designed as one credit of United States History Honors. Its integrated constitutional government and civic literacy instruction does not claim to replace Florida’s separate one-half-credit United States Government requirement.
