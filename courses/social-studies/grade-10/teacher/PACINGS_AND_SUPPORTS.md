# Pacing and Supports

## Standard pacing
Each week contains approximately 285 minutes of core instruction. The portal also supports block scheduling by combining Monday/Tuesday, Wednesday/Thursday, and Friday assessment.

## Foundation pathway
- Vocabulary preview and read-aloud
- Partially completed timelines and maps
- Chunked primary sources
- Sentence frames and oral rehearsal
- Reduced writing length without reducing the reasoning target
- Extended time and alternative response mode

## Core pathway
Complete all weekly readings, three assignments, quiz, corrections, midterm, final, and capstone.

## Extended honors pathway
- Locate and assess an additional source
- Compare historiographical interpretations
- Write a stronger counterclaim
- Conduct original local-record research
- Complete AP-style short-answer, long-essay, and document-based practice

## Recovery and reassessment
Quiz retakes are permitted after a correction form. Major reassessment should use parallel questions that measure the same standards rather than repeating memorized items.

## Suggested reporting periods
- Quarter 1: Weeks 1–9
- Quarter 2: Weeks 10–18 and midterm
- Quarter 3: Weeks 19–27
- Quarter 4: Weeks 28–36, civic capstone, and final
