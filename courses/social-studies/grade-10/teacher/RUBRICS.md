# Common Rubrics

## Four-domain assignment rubric — 20 or 30 points
### Evidence
4 — Specific, accurate, relevant, and identified.
3 — Mostly accurate and relevant, with minor gaps.
2 — General or partly inaccurate evidence.
1 — Minimal evidence.
0 — No usable evidence.

### Reasoning
4 — Explains how evidence supports the claim and addresses complexity.
3 — Clear reasoning with limited complexity.
2 — Some explanation, but mostly summary.
1 — Assertion without explanation.
0 — No reasoning.

### Historical accuracy
4 — Chronology, context, institutions, geography, and terminology are accurate.
3 — Minor error that does not change the conclusion.
2 — Several errors or weak context.
1 — Major misunderstanding.
0 — Not responsive.

### Communication
4 — Precise, organized, and appropriately cited.
3 — Generally clear.
2 — Uneven organization.
1 — Difficult to follow.
0 — Not submitted.

## Civic capstone
Add evaluation of jurisdiction, stakeholder representation, policy alternatives, rights constraints, implementation, cost, and measurable outcomes.
