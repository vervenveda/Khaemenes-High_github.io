# Khaemenes High School Online Academy
## Grade 9 Pre-Algebra — 42-Week Course Master Plan

### Course Purpose

This course is designed as a complete 42-week Grade 9 Pre-Algebra program for the Khaemenes High School Online Academy. It builds numerical fluency, algebraic reasoning, problem-solving, proportional reasoning, geometry, probability and statistics, graphing, and mathematical communication while preparing students for Algebra I.

The course is designed to work in both an online academy portal and as a printable/home-study course. Every instructional week contains:

- Weekly learning objectives
- Teacher lesson / instructional reading
- Worked examples
- Guided practice
- Independent practice
- Printable worksheet
- Weekly quiz
- Review / reflection
- Optional enrichment
- Progress tracking

Major assessments include a Quarter 2 Midterm and a Week 42 Final Examination. Laboratory activities connect mathematics to real-world situations and require students to collect, calculate, interpret, and communicate data.

---

# I. Course Structure

## Length

**42 weeks**

## Suggested Weekly Rhythm

**Day 1 — Learn:** lesson, vocabulary, examples  
**Day 2 — Practice:** guided examples and practice problems  
**Day 3 — Apply:** worksheet, problem-solving, or lab  
**Day 4 — Review:** correction, discussion, challenge problems  
**Day 5 — Assess:** weekly quiz and reflection

## Recommended Mastery Rule

A weekly quiz score of **80% or higher** demonstrates mastery. Students scoring below 80% receive a remediation/reteach pathway, complete correction work, and may retake the quiz.

## Suggested Course Grading

- Weekly Quizzes — **20%**
- Printable Worksheets / Practice Sets — **15%**
- Mathematics Labs / Projects — **15%**
- Unit / Quarter Assessments — **15%**
- Midterm Examination — **15%**
- Final Examination — **20%**

**Total — 100%**

## Suggested Letter Grades

| Percentage | Letter | Performance |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100% | A | Excellent mastery |
| 80–89% | B | Strong mastery |
| 70–79% | C | Developing / adequate mastery |
| 60–69% | D | Limited mastery; intervention needed |
| Below 60% | F | Not yet proficient |

# III. Weekly Lesson Package Template

Each of the 42 weeks should be generated as a complete lesson module using this structure:

## 1. Lesson Header
- Week number
- Unit / quarter
- Lesson title
- Estimated instructional time
- Prerequisite skills

## 2. Learning Objectives
Three to six measurable objectives.

## 3. Vocabulary
A student-friendly vocabulary list.

## 4. Mini-Lesson
Clear instructional explanation with worked examples.

## 5. Guided Practice
Teacher-supported problems with answer explanations.

## 6. Independent Practice
Student problems progressing from foundational to challenging.

## 7. Printable Worksheet
A printer-friendly student page with space for work.

## 8. Challenge Problems
Higher-order problems for students who finish early.

## 9. Real-World Application
A practical scenario connecting mathematics to daily life, science, finance, technology, or community life.

## 10. Lab / Investigation
When assigned, a hands-on or data-based investigation.

## 11. Weekly Quiz
10 questions by default, mixing computation, reasoning, and application.

## 12. Reflection
Students answer:
- What did I learn?
- What was difficult?
- What strategy helped me?
- What do I need to practice?

# XIII. Course Design Philosophy

This course should emphasize:

**Understand → Practice → Apply → Explain → Assess → Review → Master**

The goal is not merely to memorize procedures. Students should learn to explain why a mathematical process works, recognize errors, choose appropriate strategies, and use mathematics to interpret real situations.

The course is intentionally structured to bridge students from arithmetic into formal algebra and prepare them for the next level of secondary mathematics.
